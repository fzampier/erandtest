# Journal Targets

## Clinical Trials

Potentially suitable because the journal covers trial design, conduct, and statistical methods.

Current author instructions indicate:

- Original research maximum around 3500 words, excluding abstract, references, tables, and figures.
- Maximum 6 tables/figures for original research.
- LaTeX accepted, though Word is preferred.
- Manuscript should use minimal formatting.

Practical implication: develop a full V8/arXiv manuscript first, then create a compressed journal-specific version once the argument and simulations stabilize.
